Go Fish fishes for you in World of Warcraft: Forever

Three steps, about five minutes.

  1. 1

    Download Go Fish and start it while the game is open.

  2. 2

    Confirm your cast key, click Install addon, then type /reload in the game.

  3. 3

    Stand at the water, click Start fishing and switch to the game.

Click Stop in Go Fish to end the run.
